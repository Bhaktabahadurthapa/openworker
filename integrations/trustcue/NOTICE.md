# Attribution and Product Boundaries

## OpenWorker

This repository is a fork of `andrewyng/openworker` and remains subject to its MIT license and copyright notices.

OpenWorker provides the general-purpose local desktop agent, model-provider support, connectors, MCP client, schedules, files and terminal tools, and approval-gated actions.

## ChurnCue

ChurnCue is the domain-specific customer-retention intelligence service maintained at:

`Bhaktabahadurthapa/ChurnCue`

Its responsibilities include deterministic model training and evaluation, customer scoring, weekly risk comparison, evidence-backed reason codes, revenue-at-risk prioritization, and governed rescue-report generation.

## TrustCue

TrustCue is Bhakta Thapa's applied product and research concept. Its original contribution is the customer-retention use case, product workflow, governed integration between OpenWorker and ChurnCue, research framing, business model, user experience, outcome tracking, and future SaaS layer.

Do not describe OpenWorker itself as an original TrustCue implementation. Public presentations, DBA submissions, portfolio materials, and commercial documentation should preserve these boundaries.
